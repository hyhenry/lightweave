# Changelog

## 0.2.6

- Inside Home Assistant, Lightweave uses Home Assistant's own fonts and text sizes, so it looks at home there and fits more on screen.
- Inside Home Assistant, the Lightweave title bar is gone. The All on/off button is now on the Lights tab, next to Controllers.

## 0.2.5

- Switches that are off are visible again with Home Assistant themes.
- On phones, the bottom bar inside Home Assistant no longer has an extra gap beneath it.

## 0.2.4

- Inside Home Assistant, Lightweave now uses the colors of your active Home Assistant theme, light or dark, and follows it when you switch. Opened outside Home Assistant, it keeps its own colors.

## 0.2.3

- Sound reactive patterns react much more clearly: lights go dark between sounds instead of resting on the second color, and every effect gets its own tuned settings.
- New Bass Burst and Bass Stream effects fire on bass hits rather than on overall loudness. Neon Pulse and Monster Splash now use them.
- Sound patterns with two or three colors save their colors as a custom palette on the WLED controller, which shows up in WLED's palette list.
- Party Meter is now green and yellow, and Canada Beat is gone.

## 0.2.2

- Lights with their own microphone can now turn audio reactive off (and back on) in their settings.
- A light getting sound from another WLED can always be switched off, even when that WLED isn't in Lightweave.

## 0.2.1

- Lightweave now shows in the sidebar for every Home Assistant user, not just administrators.

## 0.2.0

- First Home Assistant release: opens in the sidebar, adds WLED controllers from Home Assistant, and uses Home Assistant's home location and time zone.
