# Changelog

## 0.2.2

- Lights with their own microphone can now turn audio reactive off (and back on) in their settings.
- A light getting sound from another WLED can always be switched off, even when that WLED isn't in Lightweave.

## 0.2.1

- Lightweave now shows in the sidebar for every Home Assistant user, not just administrators.

## 0.2.0

- First Home Assistant release: opens in the sidebar, adds WLED controllers from Home Assistant, and uses Home Assistant's home location and time zone.
